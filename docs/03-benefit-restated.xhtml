Summary

The programme tracker reports A$73,000.00 of savings. That figure is calculated on a margin definition that was retired at the last definition review, because it does not deduct solar feed-in credits, carries no provision for bills that will be restated, and ignores goodwill credits. Restated on the approved definition the net benefit is A$30,692.28 — an erosion of 58.0%.
