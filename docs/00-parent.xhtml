What this is

The document side of a Databricks Genie ontology demo for Red Energy, a fictional Australian energy retailer. Genie answers business questions by reasoning across governed data in Unity Catalog and the documents a business actually runs on — these pages, the linked tickets, and the email trail.

The situation

A cost-reduction programme removed a control that caught metering data problems. When the market data feed changed, nothing noticed. 71 households were billed on estimated reads instead of actual meter readings, leaving A$31,185.78 of revenue unsettled and 22 already-at-risk customers with a legitimate grievance.
