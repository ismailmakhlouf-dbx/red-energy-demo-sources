How we talk about performance

These are our own measures. The names are specific to us, so if a number is quoted without a definition, ask which one it is on.

What we call itWhat it meansTarget
Billing accuracyThe share of connection points whose latest reading is a real meter read rather than an estimate.98% or better
Estimated billing rateThe share of bills raised on an estimate instead of an actual read.1% or less
Unsettled revenueMoney we have billed on estimates that may be restated once real readings arrive. Not a loss — but not settled either.as low as possible
Collections pressureOverdue bills per 100 bills issued. We read it as a conduct measure, not just a cash one.5 or less
Unhappy contact shareThe share of customer contacts that come in negative. Read it next to contact volume: both rising on the same group is a grievance.20% or less
Customer healthA 0–100 blend of how likely a customer is to leave and how they sound when they contact us.60 or better
Energy transition take-upThe share of customers with solar, a battery or an EV charger.25% or better
Contribution marginThe approved profitability measure — charges less solar feed-in credits. The only basis approved for savings claims.grow
Gross margin (programme basis)RETIRED. Ignores feed-in credits, so it overstates margin for solar households. Still used by the cost programme tracker, which is why its savings look better than they are.do not use
