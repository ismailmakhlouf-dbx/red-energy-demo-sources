SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT. Red Energy is a fictional scenario used for a Databricks product demonstration. No real customers, people, consumption or personal data appear here. Every figure is generated from a synthetic dataset.

What happened

The market operator changed the format of the metering data feed. Inbound reads failed validation silently and the backlog went unnoticed for about a day. 87 connection points went onto substituted reads and 798 estimated reads were ingested. 71 households were billed on estimates across 142 bills.

Billing accuracy fell to 82.4% of connection points with a genuine latest read, against a 98% target, and the share of bills raised on estimates reached 4.73% against a 1% target.

Why nobody noticed

Until the cost programme, a format change showed up as a spike in a manual exception queue that somebody worked every day. The programme replaced that queue with automated acceptance rules. Those rules check that a row is well formed; they do not check that the shape of the feed is unchanged. There is no other detection in the path.

This was raised as a risk six months before it happened and closed as “won't do — covered by upstream monitoring”. Upstream monitoring confirms the file arrives. It says nothing about what is inside it.

Why it is taking so long to clear

The same programme shortened the re-read window and cut standby field crews. There is not enough capacity to re-read the affected meters inside one billing cycle, so estimated bills roll into a second cycle instead of being corrected in the first.

Actions

  1. Add a content check on the feed before automated acceptance. Not started.
  2. Suppress collections on any estimated bill. In progress.
  3. Restore enough re-read capacity to clear estimates in-cycle. Blocked on programme savings.
  4. Book 1–3 as costs of the programme, not unrelated incident spend. Disputed.
